# Research Plan (Minimal Top-Conference Track)

## Overview
This plan focuses on a single, defensible contribution and three core experiments that are sufficient for a top‑conference submission.

**Thesis**: Trading is a multi‑agent environment; strategies improve when they explicitly model other participants and act on those predictions.

---

## Core Contribution (single focus)
**ToM‑Strategic Decision Rule**
- Input: opponent prediction distribution + technical signal
- Output: final action and confidence
- Formal rule or objective that conditions on opponent predictions

---

## Research Questions (minimal)
- **RQ1**: Does ToM‑Strategic decision making improve performance vs technical and LLM baselines?
- **RQ2**: Which opponent model contributes most to performance?
- **RQ3**: What ToM depth is optimal (Level‑1/2/3)?

---

## Experiments (minimal set)

### E1: Main Performance
Compare:
- Baseline‑Tech
- Baseline‑LLM
- ToM‑Full

Metrics:
- Sharpe, Max Drawdown, Win Rate, Profit Factor

Setup:
- Assets: BTC, ETH, SOL
- Seeds: 3+
- Walk‑forward splits (true rolling)

### E2: Opponent Model Ablation
Compare:
- ToM‑Full
- ToM‑NoRetail
- ToM‑RulesOnly

### E3: Recursive Depth
Compare:
- Level‑1
- Level‑2
- Level‑3

---

## Method Summary (minimal)
1. Opponent models produce predictions
2. Aggregator fuses predictions into net direction/confidence
3. Strategic rule maps (technical signal + aggregated prediction) → action

---

## Baselines
- Baseline‑Tech: existing technical strategy
- Baseline‑LLM: LLM advice without opponent modeling

---

## Reproducibility Requirements
- Fixed seeds and logged configs
- True walk‑forward splits (no repeated periods)
- All results saved under `experiments/results/`
- Summary tables generated by script

---

## Success Criteria
- ToM‑Full beats Baseline‑Tech with p < 0.05 on Sharpe
- At least one ablation shows significant drop
- Level‑2 or Level‑3 outperforms Level‑1

---

## Minimal Timeline (6–8 weeks)
1. Weeks 1–2: opponent models + aggregator
2. Weeks 3–4: strategic rule + ToM agent
3. Weeks 5–6: run E1/E2/E3 + stats
4. Weeks 7–8: write paper + finalize figures
