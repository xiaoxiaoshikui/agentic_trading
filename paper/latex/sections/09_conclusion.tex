\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

This paper frames asynchronous alignment as a core financial multimodal learning problem in which dense market streams must be fused with sporadic external context whose value depends on when it arrives. On the current short real-news corpus, \CGCMA{} attains the highest mean downstream Sharpe among the evaluated baselines under a 5 bps cost-aware protocol ($+1.047\pm0.793$ over two seeds), with a robust matched seed-fold margin over the price-only Transformer. At the same time, TimesNet-lite remains a strong predictive baseline, and \CGCMA{}'s margin over it is positive but not statistically separated at the current sample scale. Supporting controls indicate that delayed external context should be modeled through grounding plus trust rather than treated as synchronizable side information or as a fixed recency feature. We therefore view \dataset{} as a useful event-conditioned financial AI benchmark and \CGCMA{} as a problem-driven instantiation of cost-aware asynchronous fusion, while leaving larger-seed, longer-horizon, and cross-regime validation to future work.
